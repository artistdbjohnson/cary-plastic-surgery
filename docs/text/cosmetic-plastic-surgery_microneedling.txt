Microneedling in Cary, NC
Revitalize Your Skin with Natural Collagen Stimulation for a Youthful Glow

Improved skin texture, tone, and firmness through collagen production
|

Performed by award-winning, board-certified surgeon
Model
Microneedling is a minimally invasive skin rejuvenation treatment that uses fine needles to create controlled micro-perforations in the skin, stimulating the body's natural healing response to produce collagen and elastin. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing concerns such as uneven texture, fine lines, acne scars, enlarged pores, or stretch marks due to aging, sun damage, or life changes, and it focuses on achieving gradual, natural improvements that enhance overall skin health and radiance, often transforming confidence based on insights from thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Stimulated collagen and elastin for firmer, more elastic skin.
Smoother texture with diminished fine lines, wrinkles, and pores.
Evened tone and reduced scarring for a refreshed, vibrant appearance.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a detailed skin assessment, discussion of concerns, and use of imaging to preview potential outcomes. The treatment typically lasts 30 to 60 minutes under topical numbing, with a device creating micro-channels to promote healing. Recovery allows a return to light activities within one to two days, with initial redness resolving as skin improvements develop over several weeks. Costs start around $300 to $600 per session, varying based on the area treated and any combined enhancements.

Benefits of Microneedling
From my experience, microneedling unlocks the skin's potential for self-renewal, offering improvements that build over time. Key benefits include:
Boosted collagen and elastin for firmer, plumper skin.
Refined texture with minimized pores, scars, and stretch marks.
Evened tone to fade hyperpigmentation and sun damage.
Enhanced product absorption when combined with topicals.
Gentle rejuvenation suitable for various skin types and concerns.
Long-term effects are amplified with consistent care, sustaining a healthy, radiant complexion.

Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults seeking to improve skin quality, often with mild to moderate concerns like acne scars or fine lines. Success is highest in those with:
Realistic goals for gradual improvement rather than instant change.
Good overall health; no active infections or severe sensitivities.
Commitment to sun protection and post-care for optimal results.
You should be free from conditions like keloid scarring. If deeper issues exist, we discuss combinations; not ideal for very sensitive or inflamed skin.

Microneedling, Step-by-Step
The in-office treatment uses topical numbing, lasting 30-60 minutes. Steps include:
Consultation:
Assess skin, discuss goals, select options.
Perparation:
Cleanse, apply numbing cream.
Treatment:
Glide device over skin for micro-channels.
Enhancement:
Apply serums if chosen.
Completion:
Soothe with mask; provide aftercare.
Improvements build over weeks.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery time is minimal and most patients resume normal activities in 1-2 days, with full benefits over months. Based on my protocols:
First 24-48 hours:
Avoid makeup/sun; use gentle cleanser, moisturize.
Week 1:
Redness fades; apply sunscreen diligently.
Beyond:
Maintain skincare; series for cumulative effects.
Tips for Recovery:
Hydrate internally, avoid irritants. Long-term: Annual maintenance; lifestyle protects gains.
Risks & Complications
Risks are low but commonly may include temporary redness or pinpoint bleeding. Rare risks include infection if aftercare is neglected.
We screen skin health discuss health history to minimize risks.
Benefits typically outweigh risks for well-suited candidates, with informed consent ensuring peace of mind.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does microneedling treatment take?
The microneedling treatment typically lasts between 30 and 60 minutes in our office, depending on the area and options.
What type of anesthesia is used during microneedling?
We use topical numbing cream to ensure your comfort during the microneedling treatment.
Will microneedling leave marks?
Microneedling may leave temporary redness, but this usually fades within one to two days, and there are no permanent scars.
Can I combine microneedling with other procedures?
Yes, you can combine microneedling with other treatments, such as serums or injectables for enhanced results.
How long do the results of microneedling last?
The results of microneedling last several months to years, depending on skin care and lifestyle, with series treatments for maintenance.
