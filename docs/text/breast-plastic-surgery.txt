At Cary Plastic Surgery in Cary, NC, our breast plastic surgery options are designed to help you achieve the shape, size, and confidence you desire.
As a board-certified plastic surgeon with decades of experience and thousands of patients, Dr. Donald Hanna specializes in procedures that address changes from pregnancy, aging, weight fluctuations, or personal aesthetic goals. We emphasize natural results, patient safety, and customized plans, whether restoring volume, lifting sagging tissue, or reducing discomfort.
