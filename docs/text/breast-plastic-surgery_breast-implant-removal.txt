Breast Implant Removal in Cary, NC
Restore Your Natural Look with Safe, Personalized Implant Removal
|
Complete or partial removal of breast implants for comfort and natural appearance
|
Addresses complications, personal preference, or health concerns
|
Performed by award-winning, board-certified surgeon
Model
Breast implant removal, also known as explant surgery, involves the surgical removal of breast implants to restore a natural breast appearance or address concerns such as discomfort, capsular contracture, or personal preference. This procedure is ideal for individuals who no longer wish to have implants due to changes in lifestyle, health considerations, or a desire for their original breast shape, and it focuses on achieving a balanced, natural contour that aligns with your body and goals, drawing from insights gained through thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Restored natural breast shape and feel without implants.
Relief from implant-related discomfort or complications.
Improved body confidence through a more authentic silhouette.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a thorough review of your implant history, concerns, and goals, often using imaging to preview outcomes after removal. Surgery typically lasts 1-3 hours under general anesthesia, with incisions placed to minimize additional scarring. Recovery allows a return to light activities within 1-2 weeks, with swelling subsiding over several weeks as final contours emerge. Costs start around $6,000 to $10,000, varying based on technique and any combined procedures like a lift or fat transfer.

Benefits of Breast Implant Removal Surgery
From my experience, breast implant removal offers more than just explantation... It restores comfort and authenticity.
Key benefits include:
Return to a natural breast shape and feel without implants.
Relief from implant-related discomfort or health concerns.
Improved symmetry and proportion through customized reshaping.
Greater ease in clothing and daily activities.
Boosted self-esteem; patients frequently report feeling more comfortable in their skin.
Long-term firmness is supported by healthy habits and optional lift or fat transfer.
Visit the
American Society of Plastic Surgeons website
for complete information on Breast Implant Removal Surgery.
Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy women who no longer desire implants, often due to complications, lifestyle changes, or preference for a natural look. Success peaks in those with:
Implant-related issues like capsular contracture or rupture.
Stable weight and non-smoking status for optimal healing.
Realistic goals for post-removal appearance.
You should be free from certain medical conditions.
If volume loss is a concern, we may recommend combining with fat transfer or a lift.

Technique Options
The techniques I use are personalized and I've advised on choices that balance complete removal with natural reshaping. Common options include:
Technique
Description
Ideal For
Simple Explant
Removal of breast implants only and often using existing scars to access implants.
No capsule issues or minimal scarring.
Total Capsulectomy
Removes breast implants and entire capsule with similar scarring to the original scars from the implant placement.

Explant with Lift/Fat Transfer

Breast Implant Removal Surgery, Step-by-Step
Performed in our accredited facility under general anesthesia, the surgery lasts 1-3 hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Usually through existing scars for minimal new marks.
Implant Removal:
Carefully extract implants and, if needed, the capsule.
Reshaping:
Tighten or reposition tissue for natural contour.
Closure:
Layered sutures; no drains in most cases.
You go home the same day with a surgical bra.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery is manageable—most patients return to light work in 1-2 weeks and full activities in 4-6 weeks. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest, manage swelling with pain meds, wear support bra.
Weeks 2-4:
Light activities; swelling subsides.
Month 1+:
Resume exercise; final shape at 3-6 months.
Tips from experience:
Stay hydrated, avoid heavy lifting, attend follow-ups. Long-term: Annual check-ups; results last with stable weight.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common complications include scarring, temporary sensation changes, asymmetry, or infection. Rare complications can include: hematoma or nipple issues.
We discuss all during consultation, emphasizing health optimization to reduce them. Benefits often outweigh risks for suitable candidates, but informed consent is key.
** Individual results vary

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does breast implant removal surgery take?
Breast implant removal surgery usually lasts between 1 and 3 hours at our practice, depending on the extent of removal and whether additional reshaping is performed.
Will breast implant removal leave visible scars?
Scars are inevitable but we minimize them by using existing incision sites whenever possible; they usually fade over 6-12 months, and we offer scar treatments like creams or lasers.
What are the potential risks or complications of breast implant removal?
Risks include infection, changes in sensation, asymmetry, or healing issues, but these are rare in our experience and we will cover them fully in consultation. *Individual results may vary
Can I combine breast implant removal with other procedures?
Yes, it often pairs well with a breast lift or fat transfer for optimal natural contouring.
What if I gain weight after breast implant removal surgery?
Breasts may change proportionally, but they will stay more natural than before the procedure; we advise a healthy lifestyle for best outcomes.
