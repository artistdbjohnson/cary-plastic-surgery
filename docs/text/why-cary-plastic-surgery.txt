Our Philosophy and Approach
At Cary Plastic Surgery, our philosophy centers on delivering natural, beautiful results that enhance your confidence and align with your lifestyle. We believe in a patient-first approach, where every treatment is customized to your anatomy, goals, and concerns.
Safety is paramount, with rigorous protocols to minimize risks and ensure optimal outcomes. From the initial consultation to follow-up care, we prioritize open communication, empathy, and education, empowering you to make informed decisions.
This commitment stems from years of seeing how personalized care transforms lives, fostering long-term relationships built on trust.
Expertise and Experience
Led by board-certified Dr. Donald Hanna, our practice brings decades of specialized experience in plastic surgery, having helped thousands of patients achieve their aesthetic visions. Our team stays at the forefront of advancements through ongoing training and use of proven techniques, ensuring precise, effective treatments.
Patients appreciate our focus on natural results, avoiding overdone looks while addressing specific needs like volume restoration or contour refinement. This expertise not only delivers beautiful outcomes but also minimizes complications, with a track record of high satisfaction from real patient stories.
Awards and Recognition
Cary Plastic Surgery has been honored with multiple 'Voted Best Plastic Surgery' awards in the area, reflecting our dedication to excellence and patient satisfaction.
These recognitions from peers and community highlight our consistent delivery of outstanding results and service. While awards are gratifying, our true measure of success is the positive impact on our patients' lives, as seen in their stories and feedback.

Patient-Centered Care
We put patients at the heart of everything, offering friendly, knowledgeable staff who guide you through every step with compassion. From detailed consultations that address your questions to customized aftercare plans, we ensure you feel heard and supported. Our focus on transparency includes clear explanations of procedures, risks, and recovery, helping you prepare fully. This approach has built lasting trust, with many patients returning for additional treatments or referring loved ones, knowing they'll receive the same exceptional care.
