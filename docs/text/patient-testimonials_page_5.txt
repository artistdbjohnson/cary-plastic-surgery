Choosing to maintain your youth or enhance your beauty Dr. Hanna is the ONLY DOCTOR for the job. Dr. Hanna listens, remembers who you are years later and shows a great bedside manor . His humor is calming and he truly cares for each patient . I have seen him a number of times for skin cancers and he has continued to be honest and supporting. I am always thrilled with his magic and will continue to return.
As the Raleigh and Cary area have grown we see many new plastic surgeons . They may be good but experience and years of dealing with all sorts of issues has kept Dr Hanna at the top. Before you make your final decision speak to an EXPERIENCED PLASTIC SURGEON DR. HANNA. You will get the best because he is the best .
