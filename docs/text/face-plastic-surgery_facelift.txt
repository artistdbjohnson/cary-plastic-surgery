Model
Facelift surgery, or rhytidectomy, addresses signs of aging by tightening underlying tissues, removing excess skin, and restoring volume for a refreshed, natural look. Ideal for those experiencing sagging jowls, deep creases, or loose skin due to aging, sun exposure, or weight changes, it focuses on harmony and vitality, often transforming self-confidence based on insights from thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Rejuvenated facial structure with a more defined jawline and neck.
Reduced appearance of wrinkles, folds, and sagging for a youthful glow.
Enhanced overall facial balance and self-esteem in daily interactions.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a detailed assessment of facial anatomy, goals, and imaging for visualization. Surgery typically lasts 3-5 hours under general anesthesia, with incisions strategically placed for minimal visibility. Recovery allows return to light activities in 1-2 weeks, with full results emerging over 3-6 months as swelling resolves. Costs start around $12,000-$15,000, varying by technique or combinations.

Benefits of a Facelift
From my experience, a face lift revives more than skin - it's about renewed vitality. Key benefits include:
Tightened contours for a defined jawline and smoother neck.
Diminished wrinkles and folds around mouth, eyes, and forehead.
Restored volume to combat hollowness from aging or weight loss.
Better skin texture and tone, often enhanced with adjuncts like lasers.
Heightened self-confidence; many patients feel more engaged socially.
Long-term satisfaction shines when maintaining skincare routines.
Visit the
American Society of Plastic Surgeons website
for complete information on Facelift Surgery.
Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults noticing facial aging, often in their 40s-60s but varying by lifestyle. Success is highest in those with:
Moderate sagging, jowls, or neck laxity.
Good skin elasticity and overall health; non-smokers preferred.
Stable weight to sustain results.
Realistic expectations for natural enhancement. If volume loss dominates, we may combine with fillers. Facelift surgery is not for those seeking minimal downtime.
Non-surgical options suit milder cases.

Facelift Surgery, Step-by-Step
Performed in our accredited facility under general anesthesia, the surgery lasts 3-5 hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Hidden in hairline/behind ears.
Tissue Adjustment:
Lift SMAS, reposition fat/muscles
Skin Removal:
Excise excess, re-drape smoothly.
Closure:
Fine sutures; drains if needed.
You're sent home the same day with bandages.
Recovery & Aftercare
It's important to understand that recovery is gradual. Most resume work in 2 weeks, full activities in 4-6 weeks. Aftercare protocols:
Week 1:
Rest, head elevation, manage swelling/bruising.
Weeks 2-4:
Light duties; avoid sun/strenuous moves.
Month 1+:
Exercise resume; results refine.
Tips that may aid in recovery:
Pre-op skincare, no smoking, hydrate. Long-term: Sunscreen, annual checks; results endure 10+ years with care.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks include bruising, swelling, sensation changes and asymmetry. More rare risks include hematoma or infection.
We discuss all risks and possible complications from facelift surgery during your consultation, reviewing your health history, and health habits to reduce risks. .
Benefits often outweigh risks for suitable candidates, but informed consent is key.
** Individual results vary

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does face lift surgery take?
About 3-5 hours at our practice, depending on technique and combinations.
Will face lift leave visible scars?
Scars are minimized and hidden in hairline/creases; fade over 6-12 months with treatments available.
How many follow-ups needed?
Typically 4-6 over first year, starting day after, all included.
How long do results last?
10+ years, influenced by lifestyle; maintenance helps.
Does it affect facial sensation?
Temporary changes possible, but often resolve.
