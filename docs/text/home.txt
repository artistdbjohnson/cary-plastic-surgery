Dr. Donald P. Hanna
Board-Certified, Patient-Focused
With more than 30 years of surgical experience and board certification in plastic surgery, Dr. Hanna is known for his precision, honesty, and warm bedside manner. Patients trust him not only for his surgical skill—but for how he listens.
Meet Dr. Hanna
Trusted by women across the Triangle and voted 'Best of Cary' - Dr. Hanna helps you look and feel your best, your way.
At Cary Plastic Surgery, we’ve proudly served the Triangle community for over two decades, earning recognition as one of the top practices in the area. Led by board-certified plastic surgeon Dr. Hanna, we blend medical excellence with a warm, patient-centered approach—helping each person feel confident in their own skin.
