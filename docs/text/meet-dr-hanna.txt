About Dr. Hanna
Dr. Donald Hanna is a distinguished board-certified plastic surgeon who has dedicated his career to helping patients achieve their aesthetic and functional goals with natural, beautiful results. Based in Cary, NC, he founded Cary Plastic Surgery to provide a welcoming environment where advanced techniques meet compassionate care.
With over 30 years in the field and thousands of successful procedures, Dr. Hanna is known for his meticulous attention to detail and commitment to patient satisfaction. His practice encompasses a wide range of services, from breast enhancements and body contouring to facial rejuvenation and non-invasive treatments, all tailored to enhance your unique beauty.
Professional Background and Education
Dr. Donald P. Hanna brings an exceptionally strong foundation in medicine and surgery to his practice at Cary Plastic Surgery. After completing his undergraduate studies in microbiology at the University of Michigan, he earned his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine. He then completed a rigorous five-year general surgery residency at the State University of New York Downstate Medical Center, followed by advanced fellowship training in plastic surgery at Duke University Medical Center. During this period, he also served as the Christine Kleinert Fellow in Hand Surgery at the University of Louisville, gaining specialized expertise in microsurgery and reconstructive techniques.
Dr. Hanna’s academic and professional credentials include:
Board Certification:
Double board-certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery and the American Board of General Surgery
Licensure:
Licensed to practice medicine in North Carolina and Michigan
Academic Appointments:
Served as Clinical Assistant Instructor in the Department of Surgery at SUNY Downstate Medical Center and as Instructor in the Division of Plastic, Reconstructive, and Maxillofacial Surgery at Duke University Medical Center
Specialized Training:
Completed advanced courses in basic and advanced microsurgery
Research and Scholarship:
Authored peer-reviewed publications and book chapters on topics including free vascularized joint transfers and perioperative antibiotic use in hand surgery; presented at national meetings of the American Society for Surgery of the Hand
This extensive training and ongoing commitment to surgical excellence allow Dr. Hanna to deliver the highest standard of care, combining technical precision with an artistic eye for natural results.

Philosophy of Care
Dr. Hanna's philosophy is rooted in the belief that plastic surgery should empower patients to feel confident in their own skin, emphasizing natural results over dramatic changes.
He approaches each case with empathy, listening closely to your goals and concerns to create a customized plan that respects your anatomy and lifestyle.
Safety is paramount, with rigorous protocols and state-of-the-art technology ensuring optimal outcomes. This patient-centered ethos extends to every team member, fostering a supportive environment where education and transparency build trust from the first consultation to final follow-up.

Awards and Recognition
Dr. Hanna has been honored with multiple 'Voted Best Plastic Surgery' awards in the Cary area, reflecting the trust and high regard of both patients and peers. Additional recognitions include serving as President of the Aesculapian Society during medical school and holding numerous leadership positions at local hospitals, such as Division Chief of Plastic Surgery at Rex Hospital (1999–2005), Chair and Vice-Chair of the Department of Surgery at WakeMed Cary Hospital, and multiple terms as President-Elect of the Medical Staff. These roles demonstrate his longstanding commitment to clinical excellence and medical leadership in the community.

Personal Touch and Community Involvement
Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Hanna is deeply committed to the Cary community, participating in local health initiatives and educational seminars to promote informed choices in plastic surgery. He has also given back through tutoring roles in chemistry, algebra, biochemistry, and English across various locations, including the University of Michigan, Nigeria, and Paris. This personal touch ensures that every interaction feels genuine and supportive, creating a practice environment where patients feel like valued individuals rather than just cases.
