Accessibility
General
Cary Plastic Surgery strongly believes that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence and strives to ensure that our services are accessible to people with disabilities. Toward that goal, we have invested resources to make our website https://caryplasticsurgery.com easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities.
Accessibility
Cary Plastic Surgery has taken great care to ensure our website has been built to be accessible to those who may be visually impaired or require a screen reader to access our website. We utilize several accessibility auditing tools such as “
Lighthouse
” and “
Axe
” to accomplish this. These accessibility tools allow our website to improve its compliance by conforming with the World Wide Web Consortium’s (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA success criteria.
Disclaimer
Cary Plastic Surgery continues efforts to improve the accessibility of our website and services to facilitate accessibility and ease of use for all persons, including those with disabilities. Despite our best efforts to make all pages and content on our website fully accessible, some content may not yet be fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.

Report Accessibility-Related Issue
Cary Plastic Surgery takes website accessibility seriously and appreciates your feedback. If you are experiencing accessibility issues on our website, please submit an email to: webmaster@infostarproductions.com. Please provide us with as many specific details as possible regarding your accessibility issue so we may address your concerns. Thank you.
