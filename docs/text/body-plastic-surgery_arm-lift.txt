Arm lift surgery, or brachioplasty, removes excess skin and fat from the upper arms while tightening underlying tissues to create a more contoured and toned appearance. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing sagging or loose arm skin due to significant weight loss, aging, or genetics, and it focuses on achieving natural, proportionate results that enhance overall arm aesthetics and mobility, often leading to greater self-assurance based on insights from thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Eliminated sagging skin for firmer, more defined upper arms.
Enhanced comfort and range of motion by reducing excess tissue.
Boosted confidence in clothing choices and daily activities through refined contours.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a thorough assessment of arm skin elasticity, fat distribution, and goals, often using imaging to visualize potential outcomes. The surgery typically lasts two to three hours under general anesthesia, with incisions placed discreetly to excise tissue and tighten structures. Recovery allows a return to light activities within one to two weeks, with swelling and bruising gradually resolving over several weeks to months as final shapes emerge. Costs start around $7,000 to $10,000, varying based on the extent of correction and any combined procedures.

Benefits of Arm Lift Surgery
From my experience, an arm lift revitalizes more than appearance—it's about reclaiming ease and poise in movement. Key benefits include:
Tightened contours for sleeker, more youthful upper arms.
Reduced excess skin that causes chafing or discomfort during activities.
Better arm symmetry and proportion to enhance overall body harmony.
Improved fit in clothing, allowing greater style versatility.
Elevated self-esteem; patients frequently report feeling more active and assured.
Long-term advantages are supported by healthy habits, preserving the refined shape over time.

American Society of Plastic Surgeons website

Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults with excess arm skin or fat that affects their comfort or confidence, often after weight loss or due to aging. Success is highest in those with:
Moderate to severe sagging that resists exercise or toning.
Stable weight and good overall health; non-smokers preferred for optimal healing.
Realistic goals for natural enhancement rather than perfection.
You should be free from certain medical conditions impacting recovery. If fat is the main issue without much skin laxity, liposuction alone may be suitable.

Technique Options
Techniques are personalized and I have guided thousands to approaches that balance correction with scar minimization. Options include:
Technique
Description
Ideal For
Traditional Arm Lift
Incision from underarm to elbow; removes excess skin/fat, tightens tissues. Scarring fades well along the inner arm.
Significant sagging post-weight loss.
Mini Arm Lift
Shorter incision in underarm; focuses on upper arm laxity. Recovery time is short with minimal, hidden scarring.
Mild sagging near armpit.
Extended Arm Lift
Incision extends to chest; addresses side/arm excess for full contour including flanks. Scarring is concealable.
Massive weight loss with widespread laxity.
Arm Lift with Liposuction
Adds fat suction via cannula for a smoother, defined result.
Sagging with excess fat.

Arm Lift Surgery, Step-by-Step
The surgery takes place in our accredited facility under general anesthesia, lasting two to three hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Placed along the inner arm for discretion.
Tissue Removal:
Excise excess skin and fat; use liposuction if combined.
Tightening:
Reshape underlying tissues for support.
Closure:
Layered sutures; apply dressings or garments.
You return home the same day with post-care instructions.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery emphasizes gentle care and gradual mobility with most patients able to resume light activities in one to two weeks and full routines in four to six weeks. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest arms elevated to reduce swelling; manage discomfort with meds, avoid lifting.
Weeks 2-4:
Wear compression sleeves for support; return to desk work, limit arm strain.
Month 1 and beyond:
Introduce light exercises; monitor scars with moisturizers.
Recovery Tips:
Arrange assistance for tasks, stay hydrated, quit smoking pre/post-op. Long-term: Maintain weight and incorporate arm toning for sustained firmness; annual checks advised.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks include swelling, bruising, and temporary numbness. More rare risks may involve infection or asymmetry.
We review your medical history and discuss all possibilities during arm lift surgery to mitigate risks.
Benefits typically outweigh risks for well-suited candidates, with informed consent ensuring peace of mind.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does arm lift surgery take?
About two to three hours at our practice, depending on extent and combinations.
Will arm lift leave visible scars?
Scars are along the inner arm but fade over six to twelve months; treatments available.
Can I combine arm lift with other procedures?
Yes, often with liposuction or body lifts for harmony.
How long do the results of arm lift last?
Results endure many years with stable weight; habits maintain them.
What if I gain weight after arm lift surgery?
It may affect contours; healthy living advised for longevity.
