At Cary Plastic Surgery in Cary, NC, our body plastic surgery services help reshape and rejuvenate areas affected by weight changes, aging, or life events like pregnancy. With thousands of patients over decades,
Dr. Donald Hanna, a board-certified expert, focuses on procedures that remove stubborn fat, tighten skin, and restore muscle tone for natural, functional improvements. We prioritize your unique body goals, often combining treatments for comprehensive results that enhance mobility and self-image.
