Thigh lift surgery, or thighplasty, removes excess skin and fat from the inner or outer thighs while tightening underlying tissues to create a smoother, more contoured appearance. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing sagging or loose thigh skin due to significant weight loss, aging, or genetics, and it focuses on achieving natural, proportionate results that enhance mobility and self-confidence, drawing from insights gained through thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Firmer, smoother thighs with reduced sagging and improved definition.
Enhanced comfort during physical activities and better clothing fit.
Boosted self-esteem through a more balanced lower body silhouette.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a detailed assessment of thigh skin elasticity, fat distribution, and goals, often using imaging to preview potential outcomes. The surgery typically lasts 2-4 hours under general anesthesia, with incisions placed discreetly to remove tissue and tighten structures. Recovery allows a return to light activities within 1-2 weeks, with swelling and bruising gradually resolving over several weeks to months as final contours emerge. Costs start around $8,000 to $12,000, varying based on the extent of correction and any combined procedures.

Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults with excess thigh skin or fat that affects comfort or appearance, often after major weight loss or due to aging. Success is highest in those with:
Moderate to severe sagging that resists exercise or toning.
Stable weight for at least several months to preserve results.
Good overall health and non-smoking status for optimal healing.
You should have realistic goals for natural enhancement. If fat is the primary issue without much skin laxity, liposuction alone may suffice; otherwise, we discuss combinations.

Thigh Lift Surgery, Step-by-Step
Performed in our accredited facility under general anesthesia, the surgery lasts 2-4 hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Placed discreetly in natural creases or along the thigh based on technique.
Tissue Removal:
Excise excess skin and fat; use liposuction if combined.
Tightening:
Reshape underlying tissues for support.
Closure:
Layered absorbable sutures; apply compression garments.
Outpatient procedure. You will go home the same day with guidance.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery focuses on comfort and support and most patients resume light work in 1-2 weeks and full activities in 4-6 weeks. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest with legs elevated to reduce swelling; wear compression garments continuously and manage discomfort with prescribed medications.
Weeks 2-4:
Return to desk duties; avoid strenuous exercise or heavy lifting while continuing to wear garments.
Month 1 and beyond:
Gradually resume exercise; scars begin to fade and final contours settle.
Tips from experience:
Stay hydrated, follow a balanced diet, and attend all follow-up visits. Long-term: Maintain stable weight and incorporate leg-toning exercises to preserve your smoother, firmer thighs.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized but can common include swelling, bruising, or temporary numbness. More rare complications include infection, fluid buildup, or asymmetry.
We discuss your full medical history to reduce them. Benefits often outweigh risks for suitable candidates, but informed consent is key.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does thigh lift surgery take?
Thigh lift surgery usually lasts between 2 and 4 hours at our practice, depending on the extent of correction and whether additional procedures are combined.
Will thigh lift leave visible scars?
Scars are inevitable but we minimize them by placing incisions in natural creases; they usually fade over 6-12 months, and we offer scar treatments like creams or lasers.
Will insurance cover my thigh lift procedure?
It may if medically necessary for significant skin issues; we will help gather and submit documentation for approval.
Can I combine thigh lift with other procedures?
Yes, it often pairs well with liposuction or lower body lifts for comprehensive contouring.
How long do the results of thigh lift last?
Results are long-lasting, often 10+ years, but can be affected by weight changes or aging; stable habits help maintain them.
What if I gain weight after thigh lift surgery?
Thighs may change proportionally, but they will remain more toned than before the procedure; we advise a healthy lifestyle for best outcomes.
