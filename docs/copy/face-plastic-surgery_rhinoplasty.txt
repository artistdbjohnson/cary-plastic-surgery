Rhinoplasty surgery, also known as nose reshaping or a nose job, refines the structure of the nose to enhance its appearance and function by adjusting bone, cartilage, and skin. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing concerns such as bumps on the bridge, wide nostrils, asymmetry, or breathing difficulties due to genetics, injury, or aging, and it focuses on achieving natural, harmonious results that boost overall facial balance and self-confidence, drawing from insights gained through thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Balanced facial proportions that create greater harmony and self-esteem.
Corrected structural issues leading to improved breathing and comfort.
Customized refinements that address specific aesthetic concerns for a natural look.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a thorough evaluation of your nasal structure, medical history, and goals, often using imaging tools to visualize potential outcomes. The surgery typically lasts between one and three hours under general anesthesia or sedation, with incisions placed discreetly to reshape the nose. Recovery allows a return to light activities within one to two weeks, with swelling and bruising gradually resolving over several months as final results emerge. Costs start around $8,000 to $12,000, varying based on the complexity and any combined procedures.

Technique Options
Choosing the right technique is essential. I have guided thousands through options that balance minimal invasiveness with effective results. Options include:
Technique
Description
Ideal For
Closed Rhinoplasty
Incisions inside the nostrils; reshapes cartilage and bone internally. Leaves no visible scarring with quick recovery and keeps nasal integrity.
Mild to moderate refinements without extensive changes.
Open Rhinoplasty
Incision across the columella plus internal; provides full visibility. Small scar under the nose that fades. Allows precise adjustments and better surgical control.
Significant reshaping or functional corrections.
Septorhinoplasty
Combines reshaping with septum straightening. Scarring is similar to both open and closed rhinoplasty surgeries.
Aesthetic plus breathing issues.
We use only FDA-approved methods from trusted sources, often incorporating grafts from your own tissue for natural support.
Rhinoplasty Surgery, Step-by-Step
The surgery is performed in our accredited facility under general anesthesia or sedation, lasting one to three hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Placed discreetly inside nostrils or across the columella based on technique.
Reshaping:
Adjust bone, cartilage, and skin to refine structure and improve function.
Refinement:
Add grafts if needed for support or symmetry.
Closure:
Use fine sutures; apply a splint for protection.
You return home the same day with post-operative instructions.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery focuses on comfort and gradual healing. Most patients return to light activities within one to two weeks and see full results over several months. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest with head elevated to reduce swelling; manage bruising with cold compresses.
Weeks 2-4:
Resume desk work; avoid strenuous exercise or sun exposure.
Month 1+:
Gradually return to normal routines; final contours settle as swelling resolves.
Tips that may help the recovery process:
Follow a gentle skincare routine, avoid blowing your nose forcefully, and attend all follow-ups. Long-term: Protect your nose from injury and maintain healthy habits for enduring results.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks include temporary swelling, bruising or sensation changes. More rare risks involve infection or asymmetry.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does rhinoplasty surgery take?
The procedure typically lasts one to three hours at our practice, depending on the complexity and technique.
Will rhinoplasty leave visible scars?
Scars are minimal and placed discreetly, often inside the nostrils or under the nose, fading over six to twelve months with available treatments like creams.
Can I combine rhinoplasty with other procedures?
Yes, it often pairs well with chin augmentation or eyelid surgery for enhanced facial balance.
How long do the results of rhinoplasty last?
Results are long-lasting, often for many years, but can be influenced by aging or lifestyle; healthy habits help maintain them.
Does rhinoplasty affect breathing?
It can improve breathing by correcting structural issues, but we discuss your specific needs to preserve or enhance function.
