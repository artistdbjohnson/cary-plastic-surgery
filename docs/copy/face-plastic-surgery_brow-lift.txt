Brow lift surgery, also known as forehead lift or browplasty, raises the eyebrows, smooths forehead wrinkles, and reduces the heavy or tired appearance caused by sagging brow tissue. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing brow ptosis due to aging, genetics, or repeated facial expressions, and it focuses on achieving natural, harmonious results that brighten the eyes and restore a youthful, rested look while preserving your unique facial expressions, drawing from insights gained through thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Elevated brows that create a more open and youthful eye area.
Smoother forehead and reduced horizontal wrinkles for a refreshed expression.
Improved facial balance and greater confidence in daily interactions.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a detailed facial analysis, discussion of goals, and use of imaging to preview outcomes. Surgery typically lasts 1–2 hours under general anesthesia or sedation, with incisions placed discreetly to lift and secure tissues. Recovery allows a return to light activities within 1–2 weeks, with swelling and bruising gradually resolving over several weeks as final contours emerge. Costs start around $6,000 to $9,000, varying based on technique and any combined procedures.

Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults noticing brow sagging or forehead wrinkles that create a tired or angry appearance. Success is highest in those with:
Moderate to significant brow ptosis or deep horizontal forehead lines.
Good skin elasticity and overall health; non-smokers preferred.
Stable weight and realistic goals for natural rejuvenation.

Technique Options
Techniques are personalized using approaches that balance lift with minimal scarring. Common options include:
Technique
Description
Ideal For
Endoscopic Brow Lift
Small incisions behind the hairline; uses a camera for precise lifting. Scarring includes tiny, hidden scars and a fast recovery.
Moderate sagging with good skin elasticity.
Coronal (Open) Brow Lift
Incision across the top of the scalp; direct tissue adjustment. Offers a more comprehensive lift and smoothing with scarring hidden within the hair.
Significant sagging or high forehead.
Temporal (Limited) Brow Lift
Targeted lift with incisions at the temples; lifts the outer brow. Small scars hidden in the hairline with minimal downtime.
Lateral brow droop.
Direct Brow Lift
Incision directly above the brow; removes excess skin and offers more precise elevation, with the scar fading into the brow line.
Severe brow ptosis in select patients.
We prioritize nerve preservation and natural positioning.
Brow Lift Surgery, Step-by-Step
Performed in our accredited facility under general anesthesia or sedation, the surgery lasts 1–2 hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Placed discreetly in the hairline or above the brow based on technique.
Tissue Adjustment:
Lift and secure the brow muscles and skin.
Refinement:
Smooth forehead wrinkles and reposition tissues.
Closure:
Fine sutures; apply light dressings.
Outpatient procedure. You will go home the same day with guidance.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery is manageable with most patients returning to light work in 1–2 weeks and full activities in 3–4 weeks. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest with head elevated to reduce swelling; manage mild discomfort with prescribed medications and use cold compresses.
Weeks 2–4:
Resume desk duties; avoid strenuous activity or heavy lifting while continuing to protect the incisions.
Month 1 and beyond:
Gradually return to exercise; scars continue to fade and final results settle.
Tips from experience:
Protect your skin from sun exposure, stay hydrated, and attend all follow-up visits. Long-term: Maintain healthy skincare habits and stable weight to preserve your refreshed appearance.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks and complications can include temporary swelling, bruising, or numbness. More rare risks and complications include infection, asymmetry, or hairline changes.
We discuss your full medical history to reduce these risks and complications and the benefits often outweigh risks for suitable candidates.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does brow lift surgery take?
Brow lift surgery usually lasts between 1 and 2 hours at our practice, depending on the technique and whether additional procedures are combined.
Will brow lift leave visible scars?
Scars are minimal and placed discreetly in the hairline or natural creases; they usually fade over 6–12 months, and we offer scar treatments like creams or lasers.
What are the potential risks or complications of brow lift?
Risks include temporary swelling, bruising, or numbness, but these are rare in our experience and we will cover them fully in consultation.
Will insurance cover my brow lift procedure?
It may if the procedure is medically necessary to improve vision; we will help gather and submit documentation for approval.
Can I combine brow lift with other procedures?
Yes, it often pairs well with
blepharoplasty
or
facelift
for comprehensive upper-face rejuvenation.
How long do the results of brow lift last?
Results are long-lasting, often 10+ years, but can be affected by aging or lifestyle; stable habits help maintain them.
