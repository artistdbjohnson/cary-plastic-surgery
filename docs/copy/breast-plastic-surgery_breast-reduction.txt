Breast reduction surgery, or reduction mammaplasty, removes excess fat, glandular tissue, and skin to achieve a more proportionate breast size, alleviating discomfort from overly large breasts. Ideal for those experiencing chronic pain, skin irritation, or self-image issues due to genetics, weight changes, or life events, it focuses on both physical relief and enhanced body confidence, often transforming daily life based on feedback from patients.
Key Benefits
Relief from back, neck, shoulder pain, and improved posture.
Enhanced mobility for physical activities and reduced skin rashes or bra strap grooves.
Better clothing fit and boosted self-esteem in personal and social settings.
What to Expect
Consultations include a thorough review of medical history, goals, and breast measurements to tailor the approach. Surgery typically lasts 2-4 hours under general anesthesia, involving incisions to remove tissue and reshape for natural contours. Recovery allows return to light activities in 1-2 weeks, with full resumption in 4-6 weeks as swelling subsides. Costs start around $9,000-$10,500, varying by complexity and any combined procedures.

Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery is faster than many expect. Most patients return to light work in 1-2 weeks and full activities in 4-6 weeks. A typical recovery may look like the following:
Week 1:
Rest, manage mild soreness with pain meds; avoid lifting.
Weeks 2-4:
Light activities; swelling subsides.
Month 1+:
Resume exercise; final shape at 3-6 months.
Tips from experience:
Stay hydrated, wear support bras, quit smoking pre/post-op, and attend follow-ups. Long-term: Implants not needed, but annual check-ups recommended; results last 10+ years with stable weight.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks include scarring, temporary sensation changes, asymmetry, or infection. More rare risks include hematoma or nipple issues.
We discuss all risks and possible complications from breast reduction surgery during your consultation, emphasizing smoking cessation and health optimization to reduce them.
Benefits often outweigh risks for suitable candidates, but informed consent is key.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does breast reduction surgery take?
About 2-4 hours at our practice, depending on the extent of reduction and if combined with other procedures.
Will breast reduction surgery leave visible scars?
Scars are inevitable but we minimize them with precise techniques; they usually fade over 6-12 months, and we offer scar treatments like creams or lasers.
What are the potential risks or complications of breast reduction?
Risks include infection, changes in sensation, asymmetry, or healing issues, but these are rare in our experience and we'll cover them fully in consultation.
How many follow-up appointments will I need after surgery?
Typically 4-5 appointments over the first 6 months, starting with one in the first week, to monitor your progress—all included in your procedure.
Will insurance cover my breast reduction procedure?
It may if medically necessary for pain or health issues; we'll help gather and submit documentation for approval.
Can I breastfeed after breast reduction surgery?
Many patients can, as we preserve milk ducts where possible, but it's not always guaranteed—let's discuss your family plans.
How long do the results of breast reduction last?
Results are long-lasting, often 10+ years, but can be affected by weight changes, pregnancy, or aging; stable habits help maintain them.
What if I gain weight after breast reduction surgery?
Breasts may enlarge somewhat, but they'll stay more proportionate than before; we advise a healthy lifestyle for best outcomes.
Does breast reduction affect nipple sensitivity?
There can be temporary or rare permanent changes, but sensation often improves by reducing nerve pressure from large breasts.
