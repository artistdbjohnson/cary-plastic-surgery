Tummy tuck surgery, or abdominoplasty, removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen while tightening underlying muscles to create a smoother, more toned appearance. This procedure is ideal for those with sagging skin or muscle separation from pregnancy, weight changes, or aging, focusing on both aesthetic improvement and functional benefits like better posture, often leading to enhanced confidence based on insights from thousands of patients.
Key Benefits
Flatter abdomen and improved body contour.
Strengthened core from muscle repair.
Boosted self-esteem and clothing options.
What to Expect
Consultations assess skin laxity, muscle condition, and goals with imaging for previews. Surgery lasts 2-4 hours under general anesthesia, involving incisions to excise tissue and tighten muscles. Recovery involves 2-6 weeks of gradual activity resumption as swelling fades. Costs start around $8,000-$12,000, varying by extent.

Tummy Tuck Surgery, Step-by-Step
The surgery occurs in our accredited facility under general anesthesia, lasting two to four hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Made low across the abdomen, often hip-to-hip for access.
Tissue Removal:
Excise excess skin and fat; use liposuction if combined.
Muscle Repair:
Suture separated muscles for a firmer wall.
Refinement:
Reposition the navel if needed; tighten remaining skin.
Closure:
Layered sutures; apply compression garment.
You return home the same day with post-operative instructions.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery centers on healing and support—most patients return to light work in two weeks and full activities in four to six weeks. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Rest with assistance; use pain meds, walk gently to promote circulation, manage drains if used.
Weeks 2-4:
Wear compression garments to minimize swelling; resume desk duties, avoid lifting.
Month 1 and beyond:
Gradually reintroduce exercise; monitor scars with creams.
Tips for recovery:
Arrange help for daily tasks, stay hydrated, quit smoking pre/post-op. Long-term: Maintain weight and core exercises for sustained firmness; annual check-ups recommended.
Risks & Complications
As with any surgery, risks exist but are minimized through expertise. Common risks include swelling and bruising, or temporary numbness. More rare risks may involve infection, fluid buildup, or asymmetry.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does tummy tuck surgery take?
About two to four hours at our practice, depending on extent and combinations.
Will tummy tuck leave visible scars?
Scars are low and concealable under clothing; they fade over six to twelve months with treatments available.
How many follow-up appointments will I need after surgery?
Typically four to six over the first six months, starting in the first week—all included.
Can I combine tummy tuck with other procedures?
Yes, often with liposuction or mommy makeovers for holistic results.
How long do the results of tummy tuck last?
Results endure many years with stable weight and habits; pregnancies may affect them.
What if I gain weight after tummy tuck surgery?
It may compromise contours, but remain improved; we advise healthy living.
