Blepharoplasty, or eyelid surgery, removes excess skin, fat, and muscle from the upper and lower eyelids to address drooping, puffiness, or bags that can make the eyes appear tired or aged. This procedure is ideal for individuals experiencing vision obstruction from sagging upper lids or aesthetic concerns from lower lid bags due to aging, genetics, or environmental factors, and it focuses on achieving a natural, alert appearance that enhances overall facial harmony and confidence, drawing from insights gained through thousands of patients over the years.
Key Benefits
Eliminated droopiness and bags for a more awake and youthful expression.
Enhanced peripheral vision when upper lid sagging was impairing sight.
Boosted self-esteem through subtle refinements that brighten the face naturally.
What to Expect
Consultations involve a detailed examination of eyelid structure, discussion of goals, and use of imaging to preview results. The surgery typically lasts one to two hours under local anesthesia with sedation or general anesthesia, with incisions hidden in natural creases to remove or reposition tissue. Recovery allows a return to light activities within one to two weeks, with initial swelling and bruising subsiding over several weeks to months as final contours emerge. Costs start around $4,000 to $8,000, varying based on whether upper, lower, or both eyelids are addressed and any combined procedures.

Ideal Candidates
Ideal candidates are healthy adults noticing eyelid aging that affects their appearance or daily function, often in their forties or beyond but varying by individual factors. Success is highest in those with:
Drooping upper lids, puffy lower bags, or excess skin creating a tired look.
Good skin elasticity, overall health, and non-smoking status for better healing.
Realistic expectations for subtle enhancement rather than total transformation.
You should be free from certain eye conditions like severe dryness. If brow sagging contributes, we may recommend combining with a brow lift.

Blepharoplasty Eyelid Surgery, Step-by-Step
The surgery takes place in our accredited facility under local anesthesia with sedation or general anesthesia, lasting one to two hours. Steps include:
Incision:
Made discreetly in natural creases or inside the lid.
Tissue Adjustment:
Remove or reposition excess fat, skin, and muscle.
Refinement:
Tighten underlying structures for support.
Closure:
Use fine sutures; apply ointment and light dressings.
You return home the same day with post-operative instructions.
Recovery & Aftercare
Recovery emphasizes eye protection and gradual return to normalcy—most patients resume light activities within one to two weeks and see full refinement over months. Based on my protocols:
Week 1:
Apply cold compresses to manage swelling and bruising; keep head elevated and use prescribed drops for comfort.
Weeks 2-4:
Return to work if non-strenuous; avoid heavy lifting or rubbing eyes.
Month 1 and beyond:
Resume exercise and makeup; monitor for dryness with lubricating drops.
Tips from experience that may help the recovery process:
Protect eyes from sun with sunglasses, stay hydrated, and follow-up promptly for any concerns. Long-term: Annual eye checks and consistent skincare preserve your refreshed look.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does blepharoplasty surgery take?
The procedure typically lasts one to two hours at our practice, depending on upper, lower, or both eyelids.
Will blepharoplasty leave visible scars?
Scars are minimal and concealed in natural creases, fading over six to twelve months with options like creams available.
Will insurance cover my blepharoplasty procedure?
It may if functional for vision issues; we assist with documentation for approval.
Can I combine blepharoplasty with other procedures?
Yes, it often pairs with brow lifts or facelifts for comprehensive rejuvenation.
How long do the results of blepharoplasty last?
Results are long-lasting, often ten years or more, influenced by aging; skincare helps maintain them.
Does blepharoplasty affect vision?
It can improve vision by removing obstructive skin, but we evaluate this pre-operatively.
